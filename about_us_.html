About Loretta dela Patisserie

✦   ✦   ✦
Who We Are

A Passion for Beautiful Baking

Loretta dela Patisserie is a home-based bakery in Pretoria, established in 2021 by Loretta Mothabela. Born from a deep love of baking and a desire to bring joy to people's most meaningful celebrations, the business has grown into a trusted name for exquisite custom cakes across Gauteng.

We specialise in custom celebration cakes, luxury cakes, and bento cakes — each one made to order with care, precision, and passion. Whether your occasion is intimate or grand, we craft every cake to reflect your unique story.

We offer delivery across Gauteng and collection is always available.

✦ Our Mission

To Bring Joy

To bring joy to every customer through freshly baked, high-quality, luxurious cakes that celebrate life's most special moments. We are committed to quality, consistency, and excellent service in everything we do.

✦ Our Vision

Your Go-To Bakery

To become a brand that people trust and love — known for exceptional service, high-quality ingredients, and uniquely crafted cakes. We aspire to be your first choice for every life occasion worth celebrating.

The Loretta Difference

Why Choose Us

Every cake we bake is made from scratch using premium, freshly sourced ingredients — never mass-produced, never rushed. We believe that what goes into a cake matters just as much as how it looks, and we hold ourselves to the highest standards in both.

Our cakes are custom designed from start to finish, tailored entirely to your brief. You bring the vision — the theme, the colours, the feeling you want — and we bring it to life with meticulous attention to detail and a skilled artistic eye.

We take pride in being reliable. When you place an order with us, you can trust that your cake will be ready on time, beautifully presented, and exactly as discussed. Our passion for making your moments special is at the heart of everything we create. Delivery is available throughout Gauteng, and you are always welcome to collect.
