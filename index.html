Our Story

Welcome to Loretta dela Patisserie

We are a Pretoria-based home bakery established in 2021, specialising in custom celebration cakes, luxury cakes, and bento cakes. Every creation is made by hand with the finest ingredients, designed to make your special occasions truly unforgettable.

We cater for weddings, graduations, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, and all of life's most cherished moments. Delivery is available across Gauteng, and collection is welcome.
